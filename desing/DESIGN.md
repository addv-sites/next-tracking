## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, enterprise-grade telematics and satellite tracking aesthetic. It is engineered for fleet managers, logistics directors, enterprise security leads, and individual asset owners who demand continuous real-time clarity, uncompromising reliability, and effortless control over their high-value assets.

### Personality & Tone
- **Precision & Vigilance:** Clean geometric structures and lucid hierarchy that communicate instant operational readiness, satellite connectivity, and sub-second telemetry updates.
- **Enterprise Confidence:** Lucid, distraction-free surfaces that convey security, institutional stability, and modern cloud intelligence without visual bloat.
- **Luminous Clarity:** An uncompromising pure light architecture utilizing crisp optical whites, pearl-slate surfaces, and high-impact electrical blue focal points to inspire security and forward momentum.

### Visual Style
The design system operates at the intersection of **Modern SaaS Enterprise** and **Aerospace Telematics Precision**:
- **Pure Light Architecture:** Strict avoidance of heavy dark containers or intrusive pitch-black cards; depth is achieved solely through crisp pure white canvases (#ffffff), airy background planes (#f8fafc), and featherlight slate structures (#f1f5f9).
- **Vibrant Energy Nodes:** Electric Cobalt Blue acts as the authoritative action driver, complemented by high-frequency Cyan accents to denote live connectivity, active GPS pulses, and state changes.
- **Structural Harmony:** Generous breathing room, subtle micro-borders (#e2e8f0), pill-rounded primary touchpoints, and softly diffused blue-tinted elevation shadows.

## Colors

The palette is engineered strictly for high-clarity daylight visibility, maximum contrast, and instant visual triage.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Electric Blue (`#0062FF`):** The primary brand anchor. Represents satellite uplink reliability, active control states, key conversion triggers, and focused navigation targets.
- **Secondary Cyan Glow (`#00C2FF`):** Used selectively for dynamic telemetry gradients, active live pings, status indicators, and gradient headline highlights (as seen in the "Rastreo Satelital" display treatment).
- **Tertiary Sky Deep (`#0284C7`):** Supplementary telemetry data, secondary interactive cues, and link hovers.
- **Neutral Deep Navy Slate (`#0F172A`):** The master typographic ink. High-density deep slate provides superior readability compared to true black, eliminating optical harshness while maintaining WCAG AAA contrast ratios.

### Canvas & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFFFF`):** High-prominence foreground cards, hero panels, interactive dialogs, and primary forms.
- **App/Body Background (`#F8FAFC`):** Soft pearl field providing a gentle atmospheric backdrop that offsets pure white cards without dimming the experience.
- **Subtle Substrates (`#F1F5F9`):** Inactive track backgrounds, subtle badge fills, search bars, and table header rows.
- **Surface Outline (`#E2E8F0`):** Ultra-fine architectural borders defining spatial cards and inputs.
- **Muted Ink (`#475569` / `#64748B`):** Descriptive metadata, contextual subtext, breadcrumbs, and inactive table columns.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all typographic levels. Its contemporary geometric curves, open apertures, and crisp letterforms provide exceptional legibility on high-DPI mapping interfaces, complex telemetry charts, and high-impact marketing layouts alike.

### Typographic Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Hero Title (`headline-hero`):** Features tight negative letter-spacing with an ultra-bold weight (800) in deep graphite slate (`#0F172A`). Key branded nouns use an electric linear gradient transition from `#0062FF` to `#00C2FF` to express digital acceleration.
- **Section Titles (`headline-md`, `headline-sm`):** Weighted at 700 and 600 respectively. Always maintain clean line height ratios to prevent collision across multi-line device headers.
- **Body Copy (`body-lg`, `body-md`):** Set in slate-blue (`#475569`) with generous 1.55x to 1.6x line heights to eliminate cognitive strain during extended analytical reading sessions.
- **Eyebrow Badges (`badge-caps`):** Formatted in uppercase with `0.08em` tracking. Paired with a soft background tint (`#E0F2FE`), text color `#0284C7`, and an embedded live ping dot indicator.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an adaptable 12-column responsive fluid grid system designed for panoramic dashboard experiences and structured product landing pages.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, fixed maximum canvas container of `1280px` centered with variable outer dynamic padding, `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px), and `margin: 2rem` (32px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem` (20px), and `margin: 1.5rem` (24px). Primary card grids transition from 4-columns to 2-columns.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem` (16px), and `margin: 1.25rem` (20px). Cards stack vertically to preserve touch target spacing.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Vertical rhythm strictly references the 4px / 8px scale.
- Layout sections employ spacious airiness (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) to establish a light, modern, enterprise SaaS feeling, avoiding data density clutter.
- Component-level spacing uses `space-xs` (8px) for icon-text pairing, `space-md` (16px) for inner card padding, and `space-lg` (24px) for master card boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy within the design system relies on luminous light refraction, crisp surface boundary lines, and colored ambient shadows rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Tiers
1. **Flat Base Canvas (Level 0):** Pure surface (`#F8FAFC`). Flat with no shadow. Used for global application wrappers and inactive dashboard zones.
2. **Resting Component Card (Level 1):** Solid white surface (`#FFFFFF`), an ultra-crisp border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), and a micro ambient shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02);`
3. **Elevated Card & Hover Surface (Level 2):** Elevated interactive modules, feature preview panels, and active toolbars:
   `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
   Border transitions gently to `#CBD5E1`.
4. **Primary Action Bloom (Button Level):** Primary electric blue call-to-actions utilize an active chromatic shadow tint to produce depth and vibrancy:
   `box-shadow: 0 10px 20px -3px rgba(0, 98, 255, 0.35), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(0, 98, 255, 0.2);`
5. **Overlays & Floating Modals (Level 3):** System dialogs, floating chat widgets, and dropdown menus:
   `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8);`
   With backdrop filter: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background-color: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95);`

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a modern, friendly yet precise rounded architecture (`roundedness: 3` Pill-informed logic):

- **Pill Geometry (Full Rounded):**
  - **Primary & Action Buttons:** Rendered as full pills (`border-radius: 9999px`) to emphasize touch-readiness and energetic interaction.
  - **Eyebrow Badges & Status Chips:** Fully pill-shaped with generous horizontal padding to frame live telematics metadata.
  - **Floating Action Buttons (e.g., Live Chat Asistente):** Fully pill-shaped with circular icon prefixes.
- **Card & Surface Enclosures:**
  - Standard cards and telemetry metric modules adopt `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for a soft, premium, friendly enterprise finish.
  - Modal sheets and primary hero banners use `rounded-2xl` (`2rem` / 32px) to softly frame interface boundaries.
- **Form Controls:** Text inputs, selects, and textareas utilize a balanced soft-pill geometry of `0.75rem` (12px) to guarantee optical harmony with full-pill buttons.
